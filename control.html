OBS setup

  1. Add a Browser source at 1920 × 1080 and paste the overlay URL.
  2. Put your game capture and webcam below the overlay source.
  3. Place the webcam at X 44 · Y 752, size 448 × 252.
  4. Use the screen links below in your Starting / BRB / Ending scenes.

Quick tips

  • Keep the Terminal window open — it runs the live sync.
  • Every change saves instantly; there is no “apply” button.
  • YouTube chat: add your chat URL as a separate browser source.
  • Changed your webcam position? Keep the overlay at 1920 × 1080 and only move the camera.
